# 9-Lever Humanization
## Lever 1: Perplexity Variance
Add unpredictable sentences after predictable ones.
## Lever 2: Burstiness
No 3+ consecutive sentences with similar word counts. Mix 5-word with 25-word sentences.
## Lever 3: Vocabulary Diversity (TTR)
Replace any non-technical word used 3+ times per paragraph with a synonym.
## Lever 4: Discourse Markers
Replace 70% of "Moreover/Furthermore/Additionally/However" with "But/And/So/Still" or nothing.
## Lever 5: Hedging vs Commitment
If evidence exists, make direct claims. Only hedge when genuinely uncertain.
## Lever 6: Specificity
Replace every vague quantifier ("several", "various") with specific numbers from results.
## Lever 7: Personal Voice
Add 1-2 first-person observations per section: "We were surprised..." or "The hardest part was..."
## Lever 8: Structural Variation
Start some paragraphs with evidence first, others with questions. Break topic-evidence-analysis pattern.
## Lever 9: Punctuation Patterns
Add em dashes for asides, parentheses for casual notes, rhetorical questions to open sections.
## Process: Three passes — apply levers, read aloud, fix remaining issues.
